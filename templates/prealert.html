How a held room works

The room held is the one whose doctor is predicted to be free closest to, but before, the arrival time, so it stands empty for the shortest time. Until the ambulance arrives that doctor can still call patients, but “Call next” only gives them someone whose predicted consultation ends before the ambulance does. When you press “Arrived”, the patient gets a token and goes straight into the held room. ESI 3–5 ambulances get no held room; they join the queue on arrival.
